## Summary

Complete the partially-implemented Isawa Ishi School at `simulation/schools/ishi_school.py`. The skeleton already wires up the custom VP provider (Special Ability) and the 4th Dan ring-raise/discount. Remaining work splits three ways:

1. **Bug fixes to existing skeleton** — `IshiAllyBoostListener` overrides the engine's `attack_rolled` listener wholesale (breaking interrupt cascade); restricts 3rd Dan to `AttackRolledEvent` only (misses 7 other roll types); requires unjustified formation adjacency; lacks once-per-roll guard.
2. **Net-new mechanics** — 3rd Dan ally-boost decision needs a pluggable strategy (Principle V); 5th Dan school-negation is entirely TODO and requires (a) new strategy class, (b) per-character `_school_negated_by` attribute on `Character`, (c) check at every school's `apply_*_ability` dispatch.
3. **Strategy + progression defaults** — install `PlainAttackStrategy` (default `UniversalAttackStrategy` wastes branches on double-attack/feint the Ishi can't do); keep `ReluctantParryStrategy` / `DefaultInterruptStrategy` / `HoldOneActionStrategy` defaults (school identity is *defensive Void mystic*, not melee). Revise `ISHI_PRIORITIES` so precepts maxes first, void leads ring buys (Special Ability ties max_vp to highest ring), water before fire/earth/air, fire last.

TDD-first (Principle I). Trace-annotated (Principle VII). Identity-driven (Principle VIII). Playable (Principle IX) with explicit caveat: 3rd Dan is structurally inert in 1v1 mirror — Special Ability + 5th Dan negation provide identity-engine firing; 2v2 mirror is the augmenting scenario.
